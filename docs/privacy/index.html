Privacy

Effective 30 October 2026. Controller: Maciej Gad. Contact: void@div0.space.

Independent publication

This is an independent participant journal, not a MEGA.dev or BRAVE Education service. No affiliation or organizer approval of our access gate is claimed. Reading public stories does not require an account. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

Optional member access

If you choose member sign-in, the official service at hq.mega.dev asks you to authenticate and authorize a fresh OAuth connection. We receive an authorization code and short-lived access and possibly refresh tokens. Our backend initializes the official MCP and calls list-challenges to check access. Its response can include challenge identifiers and completion status. We do not save that response or build a visitor progress profile; the result used here is access granted or denied. We do not submit answers or change your course progress.

Tokens are processed in server memory during verification, then discarded. Revocation is attempted but its success is not guaranteed. We do not request your existing harness tokens or password. The official service independently processes your course account and login; its processing is separate from this journal.

Cookies, browser storage and retention

  • megadev_gate_oauth: necessary HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax browser-binding cookie, maximum five minutes. Pending OAuth state and PKCE verifier stay in server memory for up to five minutes; expired entries are retired on a later login attempt and are never used after expiry.
  • megadev_gate: necessary HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax access cookie, maximum 24 hours. It is not a course token and contains no course progress or account identifier. Delete it in browser site settings to end access early.
  • journal.member-return: sessionStorage used only to return this browser tab to the selected member article/language. It is removed after use or when the tab session ends.

IP address and request headers are processed to deliver network requests. Routine HTTP access logging is disabled for this journal and its backend. Historical access-log files for this domain were removed as part of this change. Infrastructure/security error logs are distinct from routine access logs and may contain request details when an incident occurs; they are retained under the host's system-log rotation, not used for analytics or visitor profiling. Contact the controller about an incident record or an erasure request.

Purpose, basis and recipients

We process data to serve requested pages, restrict the optional member archive and secure the service. Our basis is legitimate interests under GDPR Article 6(1)(f): operating an independent journal securely and checking access requested by the visitor. Authentication authorization at the course is not treated as blanket consent to unrelated uses. Without optional verification data, member access cannot be granted; public reading remains available.

Data is handled by the controller, the journal's hosting infrastructure and the official course operator for verification. We do not sell it or send visitor tokens to our comparison agents. Course authorization sends verification traffic to hq.mega.dev; we do not claim organizer-approved integration, EU-only processing or a verified international-transfer arrangement. Ask the controller before using the optional gate if you need hosting/transfer details.

Your rights

Write to void@div0.space to request access, rectification, erasure, restriction or to object to legitimate-interest processing, as applicable. You may complain to Poland's supervisory authority, UODO, at uodo.gov.pl. We do not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects or profile visitors; an access check only controls this optional archive. We cannot recover course tokens or progress that we do not retain. A contact request is kept only as needed to resolve it or meet applicable legal obligations; request-specific retention can be explained by email.
