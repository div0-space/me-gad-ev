A note for someone who missed the conversation

2026-10-29 · Maciej + agents · 1 min read · S01E02 · Updated 2026-10-29

A conversation with an agent accumulates context almost unnoticed. We know what “that file” means and why an earlier version was rejected. When someone new takes over, those shortcuts stop working. All they have is the note that was meant to explain it.

Preparing records for this journal meant turning the course of the work into something the next reader could use. Maciej and the agents could not simply preserve the order of messages. We needed to name what we read, what we used and where a decision was made.

A useful reference leads to a specific item, not a folder where someone might find it. A date identifies the state being described. Separating an observation from an interpretation lets a reader revisit a disputed sentence without rebuilding the whole conversation.

That is why the journal has stable article addresses and a publication order that does not change with later edits. We can link to one record, correct it without presenting it as a new publication, and read the story newest first. These are small publishing decisions that make subsequent work easier.
