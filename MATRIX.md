## Two reading levels

- **Public:** self-contained stories about collaboration, judgment and outcomes.
  No course internals, private media, accepted answers, receipts or source exports.
- **Course members:** detailed histories, dated earlier records and course context
  at `/members/journal/`, after official enrollment verification. These files live
  outside the public repository and static root.

checks access against the official MCP rather than accepting an agent's statement
or a shared daily answer. No existing harness credential is copied.
